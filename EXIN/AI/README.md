# EXIN BCS Artificial Intelligence
## Essentials
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a methodology for using a non-human system to learn
from experience and imitate human intelligent behavior. The EXIN BCS Artificial
Intelligence Essentials exam tests a candidate’s knowledge and understanding of the
terminology and the general principles. This syllabus covers the potential benefits;
types of Artificial Intelligence; the basic process of Machine Learning (ML); the
challenges and risks associated with an AI project, and the future of AI and Humans
in work.
